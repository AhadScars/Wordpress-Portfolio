Process

From brief to a live, editable site.

A clear path so business owners know what ships, when, and what they can change after launch.

  1. 01

    Brief & sitemap

    Pages, offers, and brand refs. I map the homepage, inner pages, and the one action the site should drive.

  2. 02

    Elementor layout

    Desktop and mobile sections, type, and spacing — built in the builder the client will keep using.

  3. 03

    Shop or leads

    WooCommerce / Shopify catalog and checkout, or service pages with forms and WhatsApp/email CTAs.

  4. 04

    SEO & speed

    Headings, alt text, image weight, and plugin cleanup so the site loads and reads cleanly.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Training on how to edit text, images, and products. The site stays theirs after go-live.
